Inside the engine

Identify. Calculate. Prove.

01 · Entity resolution

Know exactly who you paid

Payment references are messy. Names are abbreviated, misspelt and inconsistent across banks and systems. Antreo's AI links each transaction to the correct business, so emissions land against the right supplier.

  • Matches across spelling, abbreviation and legal-entity variations
  • Gets sharper as more payments flow through the platform

02 · Emissions engine

The most accurate method, every time

Our hybrid engine converts each transaction into a footprint and scope classification. For every payment it uses the most specific data available, from product and supplier data down to sector averages, and records which method it used.

  • Activity-based and spend-based calculation
  • Automatic Scope 3 category classification

03 · Audit lineage

Every number can be explained

Each calculation keeps its source transaction, the match, the method and the evidence behind it. Re-run the data at any time and you get the most up-to-date answer, with a full trail showing how it was reached.

  • Audit-grade lineage from payment to disclosure
  • Fully replayable when data or factors improve
