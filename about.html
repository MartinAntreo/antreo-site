About Antreo

Making trusted ESG data affordable for every business

Antreo turns everyday transactions into regulator-ready environmental data. Because it lives inside the payment, ERP and treasury platforms companies already use, it is simple enough for SMEs and powerful enough for large enterprises.

Our story

The data was already there

Antreo began with an observation. While working with a carbon accounting provider, Antreo's founder saw that organisations, especially SMEs, had no practical way to capture and share Scope 3 emissions data.

Yet the information they needed already existed in the financial systems they used every day. Every payment says who was paid, how much and for what. Financial messaging could close the ESG data gap.

Antreo was built to be that bridge.
