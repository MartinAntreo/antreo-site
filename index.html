ESG data is still built by hand

How it works

Three steps, no questionnaires

Your business already records what it buys and who it pays. Antreo turns those records into environmental data.

Connect

Antreo plugs into your treasury, ERP or invoicing system through a pre-built connector or our API.

Enrich

AI matches each payment to the right supplier and calculates its footprint with the most accurate data available.

Report

Use the results for disclosures, customer requests and management reporting, with the evidence behind every figure.
