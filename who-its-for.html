Reporting companies

Supplier data without the chasing

Collecting and validating supplier emissions data is one of the biggest overheads in sustainability reporting. Antreo builds it from what you already pay.

  • Scope 3 from your payments. Every supplier payment is resolved and given a footprint.
  • CSRD and VSME ready. Standard reports on demand, with the evidence behind each number.
  • Insight all year. Track emissions by supplier, category and period.
